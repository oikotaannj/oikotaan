Thank you to everyone who contributed to our first-half 2026 food drive. Your
donations helped collect enough food to feed dozens of local families in need
through the Community FoodBank of New Jersey.

We are grateful for the generosity, time and shared commitment our community
showed in making sure no one has to go hungry.
