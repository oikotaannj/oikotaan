শুভ নববর্ষ — Shubho Noboborsho, Happy New Year! Join us for our Poila Boishakh
dinner, the first gathering of the Bengali new year.

The evening opens with Rabindrasangeet (songs written by the poet Rabindranath
Tagore) from our music group, followed by a short play staged by the teens,
and then dinner. Come in your new clothes.

## Menu

A full Bengali spread, cooked by the food committee rather than catered:
luchi (fried flatbread), aloor dom (spiced potato curry), shukto (a mixed
vegetable stew), macher jhol (a light fish curry), kosha mangsho (slow-cooked
spiced mutton), chatni (a sweet and tangy chutney), and mishti doi (sweetened
yogurt) to finish. A vegetarian thali (a full plate meal) is available for
anyone who asks at registration.

## Tickets

Tickets are $25 for adults and free for children under twelve. Members of the
association get a $5 discount, applied automatically when you register with the
email on your membership.
