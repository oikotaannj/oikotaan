শুভ নববর্ষ. Join us for our Poila Boishakh dinner, the first gathering of the
Bengali new year.

The evening opens with Rabindrasangeet from our music group, followed by a short
play staged by the teens, and then dinner. Come in your new clothes.

## Menu

A full Bengali spread, cooked by the food committee rather than catered:
luchi, aloor dom, shukto, macher jhol, kosha mangsho, chatni, and mishti doi to
finish. A vegetarian thali is available for anyone who asks at registration.

## Tickets

Tickets are $25 for adults and free for children under twelve. Members of the
association get a $5 discount, applied automatically when you register with the
email on your membership.
